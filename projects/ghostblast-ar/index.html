GhostBlast AR

Its Halloween time folks, lets have fun by hunting some Ghosts. Ghost Blast AR is the next level of 3D Augmented Reality(AR) shooting game.

The Halloween Ghosts surround you from all directions, they will try to hover around you and will try their best to attack you, all you need to do is defend yourself by aiming at the Ghosts by moving your mobile to your left or right and above or below and tap anywhere on the screen to shoot and kill them before they do and of course before the health or timer goes off.

The game uses the camera for the background, so you can use the front or back camera and immerse yourself and enjoy, you can also take photos, selfies with the Halloween Ghosts and share it on Facebook.

FEATURES

  • Easy to play and ideal for all ages.
  • 20 challenging levels that engage you all the time and keeps you on your toes.
  • Unlimited laser bullets.
  • Use strategy to complete levels without getting killed by ghosts.
  • Score three stars on each level by killing the ghosts in less time.
  • Game Center Leader Boards and achievements.
  • Share selfies with ghosts on Facebook.

HOW TO PLAY

Tilt or move your device to target the ghosts.

Tap anywhere on the screen to blast your ghost.
